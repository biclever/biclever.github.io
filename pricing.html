Questions

Pricing questions

What is a credit, and how much does a task cost?

A credit is a unit of agent work. The credits a task uses depend on its size: a question about a report uses a few, a larger change more. Every reply shows the credits that task used, so during the free pilot you see the real cost on your own requests. Our demo email thread shows a worked example.

Which plan is right for us?

It depends on how many requests you send. After the pilot we look at your real usage together and recommend the plan with the lower total cost. You can switch later.

What is a fixed-price project?

For larger jobs we agree a fixed price up front, from €1,000. For example: checking your reports for a BusinessObjects upgrade, a migration such as UNV to UNX, a first analysis of all your reports and universes, or importing your documentation (Word, PDF and so on) into the agent's knowledge.

Is there a limit on users?

No. Anyone you approve can write to your agent. It works on one task at a time; for more throughput you can have more than one agent.

What happens after the free pilot?

Nothing is billed unless you sign up. If you continue, we agree the plan that fits your usage.

Can you invoice us or work with a purchase order?

We invoice in euros (EUR), and payments run through Stripe. If your company needs a purchase order or another way to pay, contact us.

What access does the agent need?

A dedicated service account with the rights you choose, limited to one folder so it can't change reports outside it. Test or production, as you prefer.
