What you can ask

Everyday reporting, done for you.

New views of your numbers

A copy of a report for one entity, one quarter or one cost centre group.

New KPIs

Margin %, variance, year-on-year: added as columns, with the formula explained.

Answers about a report

What a figure includes, which filters apply, why two reports show different totals.

On production, safely

Where your reports are.

Finance reports live on the production system, and the agent can work there.

  • One folder

    Its access is restricted to one folder you choose, so it can't change reports outside it.

  • Copies first

    It works on a copy by default, and backs up the original before changing it.

  • You decide who asks

    Only the people you approve can write to the agent.

Good to know

A fast helper, not a decision maker.

The agent knows your BusinessObjects system, not your business. It builds the report; you decide what the numbers mean. AI can make mistakes, so check important figures before you rely on them.
