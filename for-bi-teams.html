Under your control

Your team stays in charge.

The agent works with the permissions you give it and tells you exactly what it did.

  • One folder

    A service account with the rights you choose, restricted to one folder.

  • Copies first

    Changes are made on a copy by default, and originals are backed up before any edit.

  • Every step logged

    Each reply lists what was created or changed, with folder, name and ID, and every task is in the agent's journal.

  • Knows your conventions

    We set up its memory with your folders, naming conventions and key reports, and it learns more with every task.
