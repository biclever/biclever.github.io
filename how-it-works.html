The problem

Small BusinessObjects jobs wait days and cost consultant hours.

"Add a column." "What does this field mean?" "Why do these two reports disagree?" Requests like these queue for a developer or a consultant, and each one is billed by the hour.

The agent picks them up straight away. It doesn't stop at telling you how: it builds or changes the report in your system and tells you where to find it.

What you can ask it

It does the work, not just give advice.

The agent knows your folders, universes and naming conventions, and learns more with every task. On Deltek Maconomy it already knows the standard BPM reporting universes: Biclever Agent for Maconomy.

Build new reports

From a short description, an attached spec or an existing report: queries, variables, tables, charts, prompts, filters, formatting.

"Create a report of registered hours per employee for January to May."
Change existing reports

Add columns, prompts, totals and formatting to reports you already have.

"Add Year and Month prompts to that report."
Answer questions

What a field means, which universe to use, why a number differs between two reports.

"Why do these two reports disagree?"
Analyse and check reports

Find unused objects, compare two versions, check that reports still run and give the same numbers after an upgrade.

"Do all Finance reports still run after the upgrade?"
Check the numbers

Verify a report's result against the data, or find out why a total looks wrong.

"The yearly total is off from the sum of the months. Why?"
Apply changes across reports

Once a change works in one report, roll the same approach out to others.

"Now do the same in the other regional reports."

How it works

Email in, finished work out.

Replies often arrive within 5 to 20 minutes, depending on the task. Your agent works on one task at a time; if your team needs more throughput, you can have more than one agent.

  1. Send a request

    Plain language by email, in English, Norwegian or another language. Attach a specification or an example if it helps.

  2. Get the reply

    Every report created or changed, with system, folder, name and ID, and anything left open.

  3. Continue the thread

    Follow-ups like "make the dates monthly" just work; the agent keeps the context.

  4. Clarify when needed

    If a request is ambiguous, it asks, and suggests options found in your system.

AI can make mistakes. Important changes may need human review before they are relied on in production.

Not a good fit

When to use something else.

The agent is fast at everyday BusinessObjects work. A few jobs are better done another way.

  • Everyday data questions

    For ad hoc queries and data analysis, Biclever MCP in your own AI assistant is much cheaper.

  • Very large jobs

    Processing every report in your system can use a lot of credits. Contact us first: a fixed-price project is usually cheaper.

  • Business decisions

    The agent knows your BusinessObjects system, not your business, your workflows or your people. Those decisions stay with you.

Safe by design

Built for an IT review.

Each customer gets a dedicated agent in an isolated workspace.

The AI never sees your passwords

A separate gatekeeper holds credentials and logs in for the agent. Each task gets a fresh session.

Originals are protected

It works on a copy by default, and backs up first if you ask it to change the original.

Only your people can use it

Mail from anyone else, or from an address that can't be verified, is ignored.

Your data stays yours

A dedicated agent with its own mailbox, memory and system access. Self-hosting in your own environment on request.

Everything is traceable

Every system call is logged and every task is recorded in the agent's journal.

You set the limits

A service account with the rights you choose, limited to one folder. Use a test or production system, as you prefer.

Getting started

From first email to go-live.

  1. Agree the scope

    Which systems and environments the agent may work on, and who may write to it.

  2. Connect your system

    You provide a dedicated service account, limited to the folder the agent may work in.

  3. Prepare its knowledge

    We set up its memory with your folders, conventions and key reports.

  4. Pilot

    A few of your users send real requests while we watch closely and tune the agent.

  5. Go live

    Your users get the address and one rule: write to it like a colleague.

Questions

Before you start

What does it cost after the pilot?

{{ a.price.monthly }} a month plus {{ a.price.credit }} per credit. Larger jobs can be a fixed-price project, from €1,000. Nothing is billed unless you sign up.

Where does my data go?

The agent runs on a dedicated server in Norway. Requests are processed by Anthropic's Claude API, and email is handled by Google Workspace. Neither uses your data to train models. Self-hosting in your own environment is available on request.

Which BusinessObjects versions work?

BI 4.3 SP3 and later, including BI 2025.

What if it can't do a task?

The agent handles everyday BusinessObjects work well, but some reports are very complex, and we can't guarantee it handles them efficiently. When it can't finish a task, it explains what's missing and keeps a note. With your permission, we review those notes to keep improving it.

What if it makes a mistake?

It works on a copy by default and backs up any original before changing it.

How are users added?

You decide who may write to the agent. Adding or removing a user takes one email to us.
